# 02 — Screens

Every screen and sheet, with the exact words on screen and the accessibility identifier of each control (UI tests find things by identifier, never by words). "Part colour": Jars orange, Bakes gold-brown (`A9751C` day / `E0AE4F` night), Recipes purple, Insights teal, Search slate blue, Settings grey-blue; each part has a text colour and a fill colour for its one full-colour button (`Area` in `Theme.swift`). Lists of "ids" use `<id>` for the record's id and `<name>` for a chosen word.

## Shell (`RootView.swift`, `AMSFermentLogApp.swift`, `Routes.swift`)

- **iPhone** (compact width): five tabs, each its own navigation stack. Tab ids `tab-home`, `tab-recipes`, `tab-new`, `tab-insights`, `tab-settings`. Titles Home, Recipes, New, Insights, Settings; marks jar, book, plus, chart, gear.
- **iPad and Mac** (not compact, always on the Mac): a side list titled "FermentLog" with rows Jars, Bakes, Recipes, Insights, Search, Settings (ids `sidebar-jars`, `sidebar-bakes`, `sidebar-recipes`, `sidebar-insights`, `sidebar-search`, `sidebar-settings`). Top right of the page: the menu "New" (`new-menu`) with New jar, New bake, New recipe.
- **Mac menus**: File › New jar ⌘N, New bake ⌥⌘N, New recipe ⇧⌘N; Edit › Search ⌘F; Help › How it works, What's new; ⌘, opens Settings in its own window; since build 2 that window follows Settings › Appearance (System / Light / Dark), not only the Mac's own look.
- **Pages pushed** (`Route`): jar, jarEdit, jarNew, recipe, recipeEdit, recipeNew, bake, bakeEdit, bakeNew, bakeInsights, search, guide, whatsNew, manageLists, safetyCopies. A notification tap, a new record's first save and Duplicate open pages through the `openRoute` notification.
- **Update first bar** (`update-first-bar`), above everything, red: "Update this device first" / "A newer version of the app saved your data. Changes made here are not saved until this device is updated in TestFlight."
- **The Undo bar** (`toast`; button `toast-undo`, word "Undo"): a capsule at the bottom, 4 seconds.
- **On returning to the app** the store syncs (`syncNow`). Appearance: `-dark` forces dark in tests, else the device's choice.

## The Full colour look (6.1, build 3 — `Look.swift`)

Nothing stored or done changes; only how read pages look. Shared pieces in `App/Sources/Look.swift`: `Hero` (head card filled with the part colour, white words), `Tile` and `TileGrid` (small fact tiles: name in small capitals, value under it), `StatTile` (number tile; Success carries a `Ring`), `Ring`, `ProgressLine` (slim line), `BarRow` (slim round bar, figure beside the name), `Segments` (the recipe time ribbon), `StepDot` (numbered circle on a thin line) and `DayHeading` (Timeline day heading).

- **Every read page** (jar, recipe, bake, Insights) opens with a head card in its part's colour (Jars orange, Bakes gold-brown, Recipes purple, Insights teal) with white words. The page behind is lightly tinted with that colour; cards have rounder corners and a soft shadow. Before: plain lists of facts on flat white boxes on beige.
- **Facts** sit in tiles (name small capitals, value under it), not one line per fact.
- **Days** are written "Wed 25 Nov" (`Human.day`; the year only when it is not this year). Edit pages and files keep date pickers and ISO days; exports are unchanged.
- **Switches** Ferments | Bakes (Insights), What I did | Taste test (Timeline) and Jars | Bakes are drawn in the part's colour, not the Mac's own accent colour.
- **Jar page**: head card = (since 6.2) `PhotoImage` of `photos.first` in a 60×76 rounded frame with a thin white edge, else the drawn jar (jarBig mark); name, big "Day N" (the start day is Day 1; `Logic.dayNumber`), state (Fermenting / In the fridge / Done), "started Wed 25 Nov", a slim line from the start day to the next taste test with "Taste test Sat 28 Nov · in 3 days" under it (`Human.fromNow`). Six tiles: Vegetable, Form, Brine, Room, Lid, Weight (+ Logged by when set). Burp block on one row: Burped, last burp, switch Burp alarm with its two times beside it. Timeline as a rail: one heading per day ("DAY 1 · WED 25 NOV"), the time once when several lines share it, a dot on a thin line for each line; filled orange dot = Did, green ring with "Taste" = taste test; tapping a line still opens "Change this line". Add form in two rows: switch, "When", and the text box with Add beside it.
- **Insights**: four number tiles (Jars, Active now, Success with a ring showing the share, Average rating); the two "Show" labels are gone and the Ferments | Bakes switch and the vegetable chooser share one row; Recommended recipe = head card + five tiles (Vegetable, Brine, Room, Days, Lid); Best jar so far has the button "Open this jar". Dot charts: bigger dots with a white edge, faint lines only for stars 1-5, "each dot is one jar" beside the heading; the brine chart shades 2-3 %. "Average rating by vegetable / form / lid" and "Problems that came up": one slim round bar per line with the figure beside the name ("Cabbage 4.0 ★"); no grid lines or axis. Bake insights: number tiles and slim bars the same way.
- **Recipe page**: head card = name, category, description, tiles "Hands-on" and "Start to finish", buttons in one row (main one white on the colour). Ingredients: amount in its own left column (`Human.amount`), Ingredients and Equipment side by side on a wide window. Method: time ribbon above the steps (one numbered piece per step, as long as the step takes; pale = wait of 120 minutes or more, full colour = shorter; only when steps have times), steps as numbered circles on a thin line with the time in a small tag.
- **Bake page**: head card = name, category, day, recipe; ratings Crust, Crumb, Flavour, Overall as slim bars with the stars beside the name.

## New jar / Edit jar (`JarFormScreen`)

Title "New jar" or "Edit jar"; Cancel (`cancel`) and Save (`save`, off while the vegetable is empty; on the Mac, since build 2, Save is a full-colour button in the part's colour — here orange, on bake pages gold-brown, on recipe pages purple — while Cancel stays a pale pill; Escape is Cancel). Parts: **Start** (only when templates exist or an AI key is set; new jars only): picker "Start from a template" with "None" (`jar-template-pick`); with a key: field "Describe it and let AI fill this in" (`jar-ai-text`), an example line, button "Build jar with AI" / "Building…" (`jar-ai-build`). **Basics** (a new jar starts with "Logged by" = the person who is logging): "Name, e.g. Spicy carrot #1" (`jar-name`), pickers "Vegetable" (`jar-vegetable`, custom field `jar-vegetable-custom`) and "Form" (`jar-form`, `jar-form-custom`, with "None"), both ending in "Add your own…"; "Start date" (`jar-start-date`); "Logged by" (`logged-by`, only when people exist). **Brine and conditions**: "Salt (g)" (`jar-salt`), "Water (ml)" (`jar-water`), the brine line (`jar-brine-line`: "Brine 2.4%" plus green "✓ in the 2–3% sweet spot" or grey "typical range is 2–3%"), "Room temperature (°C)" (`jar-room-temp`), "Jar size (ml)" (`jar-size`). **Spices**: chips (`jar-spice-<name>`), "Add your own spice" (`jar-spice-new`), "Add" (`jar-spice-add`). **Equipment**: "Vessel, e.g. Glass jar, crock" (`jar-vessel`), "Weight" (`jar-weight`), "Lid" (`jar-lid`). **Reminders**: stepper "Remind me to taste every N days" / "Taste-test reminder: off" (`jar-remind-days`, 0–60), "0 turns the taste-test reminder off for this jar."; switch "Burp alarm" (`jar-burp-alarm`) and its line (`jar-burp-caption`): "On for this jar" / "Off for this jar" / "On by itself for a plain lid" / "Off: this lid lets the gas out by itself". **Photos and notes**: "Add pictures" (`photo-add`), "Take a picture" (`photo-camera`, only when a camera exists), "Remove" under each picture (`photo-remove`), "Notes — anything worth remembering" (`jar-notes`). Last section: "Save as a template" (`jar-save-template`; asks "Name this template", field "e.g. Classic 2.5% carrots", Cancel / Save; bar "Template saved"), the line "A template keeps these settings so you can start the next jar from them."; when editing "Delete this jar" (`jar-delete`, red; asks "Delete this jar?" "You can undo it for a few seconds afterwards." with "Delete this jar" / "Keep it"; then the bar "Jar removed" + Undo). Saving remembers any new vegetable, cut or spice in the shared lists, plans the alarms again, and for a new jar opens its page after 0.4 s. If the jar vanished meanwhile: "This jar is no longer here."

Title = jar name; top right "Edit" (`jar-edit`). From the top: status pill (`jar-status`); "Taste test due <date>" (`jar-due`; deep orange once due, grey before); pictures; facts (Vegetable, Form, Brine [green in range], Room temp, Started, Lid, Weight, Logged by when set); spice tags (grey); notes (`jar-notes`). Missing jar: "This jar is no longer here." (`jar-missing`).

- **Burp** block (fermenting only; `JarBurpBlock`): "Burped" full-colour (`jar-burped`); read-out (`jar-burp-last`) "Not burped yet" or "Last burp: today 08:12 · 2 today" (the day is written out when it was not today); switch "Burp alarm" (`jar-burp-alarm`; turning it on asks for notification permission and sets the jar's own choice); caption "Rings at 08:00 and 20:00" + " — switched off for this device in Settings". - **To do — from <recipe>** (`JarPlanBlock`; only when the jar has plan steps): "N of M done" (`jar-plan-progress`); one button per step (`jar-plan-<stepid>`; selected when done) with a round tick box, title, detail, the recipe's time hint and, when done, green "done 14:05"; the line "Tick a step when you have done it — it is written into the Timeline with the time."
- **Timeline** (`JarTimelineBlock`): "Nothing logged yet." or every line (`jar-timeline-line-<id>`) with its date and time, a filled orange dot (Did) or a green ring with the word "Taste" (taste test), under one day heading (see 6.1 above); "Tap a line to change it". Add form: segmented "What I did" | "Taste test" (`jar-timeline-kind`, remembered per device), "When" (`jar-timeline-when`), text box (`jar-timeline-text`: "What did you do? One step per line…" or "How does it taste, smell and look?"), help line ("One step per line. Paste a whole list and every line becomes its own step — list numbers are removed." / "A taste test also moves the next taste-test reminder."), full-colour "Add" (`jar-timeline-add`, off when empty). With an AI key and Taste test: field "…or say it: “day 4, tangy and still crunchy”" (`jar-timeline-voice-text`) and "Add by voice" / "Adding…" (`jar-timeline-voice`).
- **Change this line** (sheet, `JarLineEditor`): tag, "Day" (`jar-line-day`), switch "Has a time" (`jar-line-has-time`), "Time" (`jar-line-time`), "Words" (`jar-line-text`), Cancel / Save, red "Delete this line" (`jar-line-delete`; asks "Delete this line?" with "Delete this line" / "Keep it").
- **Move to the fridge** (`jar-to-fridge`, fermenting only): sets the fridge day to today, no question, no Undo.
- **Outcome**: heading "Outcome"; "No verdict recorded yet." + full-colour "Record outcome" (`jar-outcome-record`); or the verdict box "Success" / "Didn’t work out" (`jar-verdict`), Taste, Sourness, Crunch, Overall as stars, problems as red tags, "Edit outcome" (`jar-outcome-edit`). - **Actions**: "Duplicate" (`jar-duplicate`), "Share" (`jar-share`; subject "FermentLog jar"), "Save steps as a recipe" (`jar-steps-to-recipe`; only with a Did line that is not a burp), with an AI key "AI tips" / "Thinking…" (`jar-ai-tips`) → block "Suggested tips" with "Add to notes" (`jar-tips-add`) and "Dismiss" (`jar-tips-dismiss`).
- **Share text**: first line "<title> — <status>", then Vegetable, Brine (with grams and ml), Room temp, Equipment, Started (and fridge day), Result (stars, "(success)"), Notes, and a "Timeline:" with "<date time> · Did|Taste · <words>".

## How it works and What's new

`GuideScreen` (`guide-screen`): one block per `Guide.howItWorks` section, heading in the Settings colour (`guide-section-<title>`); lines starting "• " are bullets. `WhatsNewScreen` (`whatsnew-screen`): per release a pill with the version, the date, and one bullet per line (`whatsnew-<version>`).

## Notifications (`Alarms.swift`)

Categories: "BURP" with the action "Burped" (id `BURPED`); "TASTE" (no action). Request ids `burp.<jarid>.<yyyyMMddHHmm>` and `taste.<jarid>.<yyyy-MM-dd>`; thread = the jar. Titles and bodies: see `03-rules.md`. While the app is open, notifications show as banner, list and sound. Tapping one opens the jar page.
