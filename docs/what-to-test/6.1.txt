What to look at in 6.1 (build 4) — the new look "Full colour", and the start day now counts as Day 1. Check each point on iPhone, iPad and Mac, by day and by night.
1. A jar you start today says Day 1: on its page (the big "Day N"), in the list ("Fermenting · day 1") and in the Timeline ("DAY 1 · ..."). A jar started two days ago says Day 3. The burp and taste-test notification words use the same count ("Morning burp · day 3", "Day 3 — time to taste ..."). Insights and the spreadsheet still count whole days fermented (start Monday, fridge next Monday = 7 days).
2. iPhone, Insights: the headings "Recommended recipe" and "Rating and brine strength" stay on one line, with the small note under them when both do not fit. On iPad and Mac the note stands beside the heading.
3. Open a jar. The top is an orange card with a drawn jar, the name, a big "Day N", its state, "started Wed 25 Nov" and a slim line with "Taste test ... · in 3 days" under it. The words are white and easy to read.
4. Under it: six small tiles (Vegetable, Form, Brine, Room, Lid, Weight). The Burp block is one row: Burped, last burp, and the Burp alarm switch with its two times beside it.
5. Timeline: one heading per day ("DAY 1 · WED 25 NOV"), a dot on a thin line for each line. A filled orange dot = something you did; a green ring with "Taste" = a taste test. Tap a line: "Change this line" still opens. Add a line: the switch and "When" in one row, the text box with Add beside it in the other.
6. Insights: four number tiles (Success has a small ring). Ferments/Bakes and the vegetable chooser on one row, no word "Show". Recommended recipe is a head card with five tiles; Best jar so far has the button "Open this jar".
7. Insights charts: big dots with a white edge, faint lines only for stars 1 to 5, a shaded 2-3 % band in the brine chart. The average-rating charts and "Problems that came up" are slim round bars with the figure beside the name. Check Bake insights the same way.
8. Open a recipe: purple head card with two tiles and the buttons in one row. Ingredients have the amount in its own column; on the iPad and Mac, Ingredients and Equipment stand side by side. With times on the steps, a ribbon above the Method (pale = 2 hours or more); the steps are numbered circles on a line.
9. Open a bake: gold-brown head card; Crust, Crumb, Flavour, Overall as slim bars with stars beside the name.
10. Days read "Wed 25 Nov" on read pages; the pages where you change something still have their date pickers.
11. The switches Ferments/Bakes, What I did/Taste test and Jars/Bakes are in the colour of their part, not yellow (Mac).
12. Settings, How it works: the jar page, Timeline, Insights, recipe page and "Signs in this app" tell the new look. Settings, What's new: 6.1 (build 4) is at the top.
13. Tell me anything that looks wrong, is cut off, too small to read, or has a word you do not understand.
