What to look at in 6.2 (build 5) — three number tiles on the Jars list, and the jar's own picture on its page. Check each point on iPhone, iPad and Mac, by day and by night.
1. Jars list, top: three small tiles side by side with a big number each. FERMENTING is orange with white words, IN THE FRIDGE is pale blue with a blue number, DONE is pale green with a green number. The numbers match the jars below (before: one line of words "2 fermenting · 1 in the fridge · 1 done"). On the iPhone nothing is cut off.
2. Under the tiles: the full-colour button Start a jar, and the box "1 jar is due for a taste test" when a jar is due.
3. The tile colours are the same as the pills on the rows: orange Fermenting, blue In fridge, green Done.
4. Open a jar that has pictures: on the orange head card its first picture stands in a rounded frame, where the drawn jar was. The row of pictures under the card still shows all of them.
5. Open a jar without pictures: the head card still shows the drawn jar.
6. Back in the list: each row still shows the jar's first picture as its small picture.
7. Settings, How it works: Your jars, A jar's page and Signs in this app tell the new look. Settings, What's new: 6.2 (build 5) is at the top.
8. Tell me anything that looks wrong, is cut off, too small to read, or has a word you do not understand.
