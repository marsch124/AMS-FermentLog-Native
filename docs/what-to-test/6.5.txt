What to look at in 6.5 (build 8) — every Burped burps, with 10 burps to choose from. Check on iPhone and iPad (and on the Mac if "Ring on this Mac" is on). Put the ring/silent switch on ring.

A. The list in Settings
1. Settings: right under Burp alarm there is a new part, Burp sounds. A note on top, then 10 rows: Classic, Short pop, Deep rumble, Long one, Squeaky, Double, Gurgle, Rising, Tiny, Big one. Each has a Play button, a small line under its name and a switch. All 10 are on.
2. Tap Play on each one. You hear that burp at once. Are they different from each other? Too loud, too long, not funny enough?
3. With the switch on silent, tap Play: you still hear it. That is meant.
4. Under the list: "All off: Burped makes no sound." and "Your choice is kept on this device; ..."
5. Settings, Burp alarm: the note ends "...and plays a burp."

B. Burped on a jar's page
6. Open a fermenting jar and tap the orange Burped button. You hear a burp. The bar "Burp logged 08:12" with Undo shows as before.
7. Tap it five times. The burps change and you never hear the same one twice in a row.
8. Switch most burps off in Settings, leaving two. Burped now plays only those two, taking turns. Leave only one: that one every time.
9. Switch all off. Burped is quiet. Switch them back on.

C. Burped on a reminder
10. Set Evening to a minute ahead (Settings, Burp alarm) and wait. Close FermentLog so it is not on the screen.
11. Press and hold the reminder (or pull it down) and tap Burped. You hear a burp and a small note shows: "Burped: <jar name>" and "Written in the Timeline at 08:12. Burp: <name>." The name is the burp you heard. The note goes away by itself after about 8 seconds.
12. Open the jar: the Timeline has "Burped the jar" with the time.
13. With FermentLog open on the screen when you press Burped: you hear the burp, no note shows.
14. With all burps off: no sound and no note.

D. Silent
15. With the switch on silent, or a Focus that silences FermentLog: Burped on the page and on the reminder make no burp. That is meant.

E. Words
16. Settings, What's new: 6.5 (build 8) is at the top. How it works, The burp alarm, has the burp sound lines.
