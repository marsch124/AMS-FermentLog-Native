FermentLog 6.7 (build 10) - what to test

New: pictures on any line of a jar's Timeline (What I did and Taste test). Tap a picture to see the jar's photo story.

1. Update the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac to 6.7 first. (A device still on 6.6 says "Update this device first" and saves nothing.)
2. Open a jar. In the Timeline, choose Taste test, write "tangy", tap Take a picture (iPhone or iPad), then Add. The line shows the picture under its words.
3. Add a picture alone, with no words (What I did). The line says "Picture" in grey with the picture under it.
4. Add words and a picture together. Then paste 3 lines in What I did and add 1 picture: the picture should sit on the last line only.
5. Tap a picture: the photo story opens. Swipe left and right (Mac: Previous, Next or the arrow keys). Check "3 of 8" at the top and the caption at the bottom, for example "Day 3 · Wed 25 Nov 08:12 - Taste: tangy". A picture-only line has no words in its caption. Done (Escape on the Mac) closes it.
6. Tap a line's words: Change this line. See the Pictures section; add one, remove one (Remove), Save. Also try a line with only pictures and no words: Save should work.
7. The jar's orange head card and the small picture in the Jars list show the newest Timeline picture. A jar with no Timeline pictures still shows its own first picture.
8. The note beside Timeline says "... tap a line to change it, a picture to see it large" when a line has a picture.
9. Take a picture on one device: it shows on the other devices after a moment.

Also check:
- Edit jar > Photos and the picture row under the head card work as before.
- Share on a jar says "(1 picture)" after a line with a picture.
- Burped, the To do list and the taste-test reminder behave as before.
